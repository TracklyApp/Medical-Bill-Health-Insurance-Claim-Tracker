01. Quick start

TracklyApp keeps medical bills, insurance responses, payments and follow-ups together on your device. The interface and examples are in English. Amounts are recorded in USD.

  1. Open Start TracklyApp.cmd in the application folder on Windows. In Chrome, use http://localhost:8765/. Keep the local server running while using this address.
  2. Explore the seven fictional bills. The Sample workspace label identifies example data; it is separate from the demo usage allowance.
  3. Open Family & Planning > Family Members and review Alex Morgan and Jamie Morgan. Use the family selector to view one person's records.
  4. To begin your own workspace, download a backup if needed, then choose Settings > Start with empty records. Confirm only when you are ready to replace the examples.
  5. Add your family members and insurance plan, then add a medical bill. Enter information from the actual bill and EOB.
  6. Record payments separately, attach documents, review deadlines and create your first report.

Example: Westbrook Medical Center billed $4,850. The insurer allowed $2,900 and paid $2,200. If the EOB explicitly assigns $700 to the patient and there are no patient payments, Still Owed is $700.

02. Navigation, profile and appearance

Open a sidebar category to reveal its pages. On a narrow phone or tablet screen, tap Menu, open a category and select a page. The menu closes after selection.

  • Family & Planning: Family Members, Action Center and Calendar.
  • Bills & Insurance: Medical Bills, Insurance Claims, EOB Tracker, Bill vs. EOB, Coverage, Denials & Appeals and Claim Timeline.
  • Payments: Payments, Payment Plans and HSA / FSA.
  • Documents & Tools: Document Scanner, Private Vault, Contacts, Reports and User Manual.
  • Settings: backups, appearance, installation, examples and activation.

Select the circular profile button at the top to edit the workspace profile. Its initial comes from the saved display name. This is a local profile, not an online account. Use the sun/moon button for light or dark mode, or adjust appearance and text size in Settings. Report PDFs always use a white, printer-friendly layout.

03. Demo allowance and activation

The demo allows 20 new medical bills. The seven supplied sample bills do not count. A bill displayed in Medical Bills, Insurance Claims and EOB Tracker is one record, not three separate entries.

  • Saving a new bill manually or from a reviewed scan uses one entry.
  • Importing a backup containing previously unseen, non-sample bill IDs uses entries for those bills. An import that exceeds the remaining allowance is rejected as a whole.
  • Editing an existing bill does not use another entry. Family profiles, contacts, payments, documents, actions and timeline events do not consume bill entries.
  • Deleting a bill, starting fresh or reloading samples does not refund used entries. Restoring a previously counted bill with the same ID does not count it twice.
  • After 20 entries, existing records remain accessible. Editing, deleting, related payments and documents, reports and backups remain available. Additional new bills require activation.
  1. Obtain the activation code supplied with your purchase.
  2. Choose Activate app in the demo banner, or open Settings > Activation > Enter activation code.
  3. Paste the full code and save. Spaces and hyphens are accepted.
  4. Settings shows Activated; you can add additional bills.

Activation is stored for this browser and site address. Activate separately on another device or browser. Keep the purchase code: activation is not included in a medical-data backup. There is no automatic online purchase verification or account sign-in.

04. Family members and patient selection

  1. Open Family Members > Add family member.
  2. Enter the full name, choose a relationship and profile color, and optionally select a photo.
  3. Save. The family card shows linked bills and the currently recorded amount still owed.
  4. When adding a bill, choose the patient from the Patient name dropdown. Select Add another patient if the name is not listed.
  5. Use View records on a family card, or the Viewing selector on supported pages, to filter records.

Example: Assign a dental bill to Alex Morgan and a laboratory bill to Jamie Morgan. Selecting Alex limits the dashboard and related records to Alex's bills. Reports have their own family selector; check it before exporting.

Removing a family profile does not mean deleting the person's medical bills. Review the confirmation and keep a backup before changing family links. An unlinked patient name can still be kept on a bill.

05. Medical bills: step by step

  1. Open Medical Bills > Add medical bill.
  2. Enter the provider or facility, select a patient and choose the provider type, such as Hospital, Dentist or Laboratory.
  3. Enter the service date and bill due date. The service date controls report period filtering.
  4. Link the appropriate insurance plan when available.
  5. Enter the original billed amount and insurance paid. Enter the allowed amount, EOB patient responsibility and current provider balance only when known.
  6. Add the claim number, status, EOB reference and EOB date. Add any denial reason, appeal deadline and claim notes.
  7. Add supporting photos if available, then save.

Example: For a $360 laboratory bill with no insurance response, enter Billed $360, Insurance Paid $0 and Pending. Leave allowed amount, EOB responsibility and provider balance blank when unknown. A blank responsibility is shown as unconfirmed rather than assumed to be $0.

Use search to find a provider, patient or claim. Use the status filter to narrow results. Editing the bill updates the same underlying record in Insurance Claims and EOB Tracker.

06. Understanding amounts and dashboard totals

FieldWhat to enter or expect
BilledThe original provider charge before insurance adjustments.
AllowedThe amount the insurer recognizes, as stated on the EOB.
Insurance PaidThe recorded insurer payment to the provider.
EOB Patient ResponsibilityThe amount explicitly assigned to the patient on the EOB.
You PaidRecorded patient payments minus provider refunds.
Still OwedRecorded EOB patient responsibility minus net patient payments, with a minimum of zero.
Provider BalanceThe provider's current balance after payments, entered separately for comparison.

Totals reflect the records and family currently selected. Bills with unknown patient responsibility are counted separately and excluded from Still Owed. Their balance is not treated as confirmed zero.

Example: EOB responsibility $700 minus a $200 patient payment gives $500 Still Owed. A later $50 provider refund changes net patient payments to $150 and Still Owed to $550. A $50 HSA reimbursement does not change the provider debt.

The app does not derive patient responsibility from Billed minus Insurance Paid, or from Allowed minus Insurance Paid. Enter the responsibility shown on the EOB. An overpayment may produce a credit instead of a negative amount owed.

07. Insurance claims and EOB review

Insurance Claims and EOB Tracker are views of your medical bill records. Enter and update the claim information on the bill.

  • Pending: waiting for an insurer's response.
  • Approved: the recorded claim has been approved.
  • Partially Paid: a partial insurer payment has been recorded.
  • Denied: the insurer has rejected the claim according to the response you received.
  1. Open Bill vs. EOB and choose the bill.
  2. Compare the recorded billed amount, allowed amount, insurance payment and patient responsibility.
  3. Open an attached EOB or bill photo for reference.
  4. Compare the current provider balance with the remaining EOB-based responsibility.
  5. If the figures differ, check missing payments, adjustments and the latest provider statement; add a follow-up action or note.

Example: A provider balance of $2,650 alongside an EOB responsibility of $700 and no patient payments deserves review. Review required is a prompt to investigate the recorded figures; it does not automatically establish that the provider made an error.

08. Coverage, deductible and out-of-pocket maximum

  1. Open Coverage and add an insurance plan.
  2. Record the plan name, coverage year, member details and the annual deductible and out-of-pocket limits.
  3. Enter the deductible used and out-of-pocket amount used from the insurer's current records.
  4. Record when the amounts were confirmed and add useful notes.
  5. Link bills to the plan so you can understand which coverage applies.

Example: A $2,000 deductible with $1,250 used shows $750 remaining. A $6,000 out-of-pocket limit with $2,480 used shows $3,520 remaining.

These trackers use the plan values you record. Payments are not automatically assumed to count toward a deductible or out-of-pocket maximum. Update the values when your insurer confirms them, especially at the start of a new coverage year.

09. Denials, appeals and claim timeline

  1. Set the claim status to Denied when the actual insurer response says so.
  2. Enter the denial reason and the appeal deadline from that response.
  3. Open Denials & Appeals and record the action taken, date, reference number and notes.
  4. Attach supporting documents and keep a record of calls, mailed letters and submission confirmations.
  5. Update the claim and appeal status when a response arrives. Mark an appeal closed when appropriate.

Example: Add “Called insurer” with reference DEMO-REF, then “Requested missing documentation from provider.” Add a task to gather the EOB, itemized bill and response letter before the recorded deadline.

Claim Timeline combines dated bill activity and recorded events. Choose a bill, add an event and optionally link a supporting document belonging to that bill. Use notes for a representative's name and the exact next step agreed. A saved deadline is a reminder you entered, not a deadline calculated from insurance rules.

10. Payments, refunds and reimbursements

  1. Open Payments and add a transaction linked to the correct medical bill.
  2. Select Payment, Provider refund or Reimbursement.
  3. Enter the transaction date, amount, account, reference and notes.
  4. Save and check the bill's remaining amount.

Payment reduces the patient's outstanding responsibility. Provider refund reduces net patient payments and can increase the amount still owed. Reimbursement records money returned from another source and does not reduce the provider balance a second time.

Example: Record a $200 payment to a dentist from an HSA account with its receipt reference. If the HSA later reimburses a separately paid expense, record that as a reimbursement rather than a second provider payment. Check the selected bill carefully to avoid assigning money to a different visit.

11. Payment plans and HSA / FSA

In Payment Plans, add the related bill, agreed total, installment amount, frequency and start date. Record actual installments as Payment transactions against that bill; creating a plan alone does not record a payment.

Example: A $700 agreement paid in $175 monthly installments has four installments before any changes. After recording the first $175 payment, review the remaining amount and next due date. Existing payments associated with the bill can affect the displayed plan progress, so check the agreement against the actual transactions.

In HSA / FSA, review transactions assigned to those accounts and the eligibility information you recorded. Keep receipts and reimbursement references. Eligibility flags are your own record and are not a determination of tax or plan eligibility.

12. Action Center and Calendar

Action Center combines follow-ups from your records with tasks you add yourself. Filter Open, Done or All; set a priority and record a concrete next step.

  1. Add a task such as “Request itemized hospital bill.”
  2. Link it to a bill, choose a due date and add notes.
  3. Mark it done when complete. Reopen it if further work is needed.
  4. Open Calendar to review bill due dates, appeal deadlines and scheduled plan payments.

Example: Create a high-priority task two days before the appeal deadline to check that the submission was received.

The app displays reminders when you open it. It does not send emails, SMS messages or guaranteed background notifications. Check the calendar regularly and add essential deadlines to your device calendar if needed.

13. Photos and supporting documents

Bill photos can show the first image as a small thumbnail in record lists. Open a photo to see a larger preview, move between images or download it. Use clear, upright images and avoid cutting off the bill's totals or reference numbers.

  1. Edit a bill to add its photos, or use Add document in the document tools to store a supporting file.
  2. Select the related bill and document type: Bill, EOB, Receipt, Appeal or Other.
  3. Add a date and descriptive notes, then save.
  4. For PDF reports, explicitly select which documents to append.

Document uploads accept PDF, JPG, PNG and WebP files up to 25 MB. The scanner accepts an image up to 12 MB. Photos may be optimized for local storage. Keep originals separately if you need their original resolution or file metadata.

Documents are stored locally with the workspace. An attachment is not automatically sent to a provider or insurer. Selected report attachments are fitted to A4 pages; original files remain unchanged.

14. Document Scanner

  1. Open Document Scanner.
  2. Choose a clear photo of an English medical bill, or select Try sample bill.
  3. Wait for local text recognition to complete. The first use may need the scanner assets to finish caching.
  4. Read the recognized text and select Review & create bill.
  5. Check the provider, patient, date and every amount. Correct mistakes and leave unknown EOB responsibility blank.
  6. Save only after reviewing. This creates a new bill and uses one demo entry unless the app is activated.

Text recognition can misread digits, dates and labels. It proposes values; it does not confirm insurance benefits or submit a claim. If recognition fails, try a sharper image with even lighting or add the bill manually.

15. Contacts

Open Contacts to add provider billing departments and insurance support details. Store the organization name, contact type, phone, email, address and notes when available.

Example: “Westbrook Medical Center - billing department. Ask for an itemized statement and mention claim CLM-001.” Record call-specific history in the bill's timeline or appeal history so it remains associated with that claim.

For filtered full reports, contact matching uses provider and plan names. Use consistent organization names and verify the final report contains the contacts you intended.

16. Full reports and time periods

  1. Open Documents & Tools > Reports.
  2. Choose Full report for all supported details, or the available selection mode to choose individual bills.
  3. Choose All family members or one family member.
  4. Select the time period: All time, This month, Last month, This year or Custom dates. For a custom period, enter the start and end service dates.
  5. Review the selected record count and totals. The end date cannot precede the start date.
  6. Choose the documents to append. In selection mode, review the bill and content checkboxes too.
  7. Choose Download PDF or Print report.

Both endpoints of a custom period are included. This month and This year run through today. Last month covers the complete previous calendar month.

Example: September 1 to September 30 includes bills whose service dates fall in September. A payment made in October for a selected September bill remains in that bill's linked history. A payment in September for an August bill is not included merely because its payment date is in September.

Full reports include the financial summary, detailed bills, claims and EOB information, notes, timelines, related payments, refunds and reimbursements, family details, linked coverage, appeals, payment plans, HSA/FSA entries, actions, matching contacts and selected attachments. Scope is based on selected bills and family; some general household data is shown only for an unrestricted workspace report.

Balances reflect currently recorded information. A report for a past service period is not a historical reconstruction of what was owed on the end date. Use the preview and report labels to confirm scope before sharing.

17. Printing and saving as PDF

  1. Choose Print report in Reports. Wait for the PDF preview to load.
  2. If Chrome does not open printing automatically, click Print above the preview or use the PDF viewer's printer icon.
  3. Select your printer or Save as PDF.
  4. Choose A4, portrait, one page per sheet and 100% scale. Use Fit to printable area if your printer requires it.
  5. Check the preview and selected pages before printing.

The report uses a white background, dark text, light table headings, margins of approximately 15 mm, repeated table headers and numbered pages. It is independent of the app's light or dark theme. Supporting documents are fitted inside an A4 page and may retain their original colors.

To print this manual, open User Manual.html from the application folder or choose Open printable manual in the app. Press Ctrl+P on Windows or Command+P on macOS. Expand any sections you want visible if printing directly from the application interface.

18. Private Vault, backups and restore

Workspace encryption: Open Private Vault, enable encryption and choose a passphrase of at least 10 characters. Confirm it carefully. The vault protects saved records with your passphrase and can be locked manually; an unlocked encrypted workspace also locks after inactivity.

The passphrase is not stored for recovery. Keep it somewhere you can retrieve. The activation code and vault passphrase serve different purposes: an activation code does not unlock encrypted medical records.

  1. Open Settings > Download backup.
  2. If your workspace is not encrypted, choose and confirm a backup passphrase. If it is encrypted, the backup uses the existing encrypted payload.
  3. Save the .trackly file in a location you control. Keep the passphrase separately.
  4. To restore, choose Restore backup, select the backup file and enter its passphrase when asked.
  5. Review the replacement confirmation. Restoring replaces the workspace rather than merging records.

Backups include medical records, family profiles, documents and photos. They do not include activation status or the trial allowance. An import containing additional new bill IDs is subject to the demo allowance. Export a backup before deleting records, resetting a workspace, moving browsers or clearing site data.

19. Offline use, installation and devices

After the initial load from localhost or HTTPS, the app caches its application files for offline use. In Settings, choose Check offline readiness. Complete the first load before relying on the scanner or PDF export without a connection.

  • Windows/Chrome: launch the local server, open localhost:8765 and use Install app when offered.
  • Android: open an HTTPS-hosted copy in Chrome and use the browser's installation option when supported.
  • iPhone/iPad: open an HTTPS-hosted copy in Safari and use Share > Add to Home Screen.

The supplied Windows server listens only on this computer. A phone's localhost address refers to the phone, not your PC. To install on another device, the same app files need an HTTPS address accessible to that device.

Each browser, device and site address has separate local records. There is no automatic cloud sync. Transfer data with an encrypted backup and restore it on the destination device. Opening index.html directly as a file does not provide the same installation and service-worker behavior as localhost or HTTPS.

20. Troubleshooting and a complete practice workflow

ProblemWhat to check
Menus do not fitUse Menu on small screens, then open the relevant category. Scroll inside the navigation when needed.
Amounts seem wrongCheck the selected family, EOB responsibility, duplicate payments, provider refunds and unknown values.
Some bills are missing from a reportCheck the family, service-date period and record selection. Full mode includes all matching bills.
Print does not openUse the preview's printer icon or download the PDF and print it from Chrome.
Cannot add another billCheck the demo counter and activation. Storage or a locked vault may also prevent saving.
New update is not visibleWhile connected to the local server, reload with Ctrl+Shift+R, allow the update to finish, then reopen the app.
Stored records cannot be readUse Export original data if offered. Preserve the original before restoring a known valid backup.
Forgot the vault passphraseIt cannot be recovered through an activation code. Look for your saved passphrase or a usable backup and its passphrase.

Practice workflow: Select Alex Morgan, review the fictional hospital bill, compare its EOB, record a fictional $100 payment, add a follow-up task, attach a receipt and export a full report for the service month. Verify that the payment changes the remaining responsibility by $100. Then download an encrypted backup and keep its passphrase.

Sample records and images are fictional demonstrations. TracklyApp organizes information you enter; it does not contact providers, file claims, submit appeals or determine coverage. Review the original documents when deciding what to pay or contest.
